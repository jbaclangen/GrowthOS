## 3. Positioning statement

> **For** owners and small teams at service businesses who are spending time and money on marketing without a clear plan,
> **who** need to know what to work on next and need help getting that work done,
> **Braventa is** a business growth system
> **that** learns how your business works, tells you which opportunities matter most, turns them into a plan, and drafts the work for your approval — then, as connections ship, carries that work into the tools you already use and measures what it achieved.
> **Unlike** AI writing tools, schedulers, or a patchwork of agencies and freelancers, Braventa starts from your business context and keeps strategy, production, and (as it grows) execution and results in one accountable system.

Short form for internal alignment: *Braventa is the one system that knows your business, decides what matters next, and helps you get it done.*

## 4. The problem

Small and growing service businesses do not lack tools. They lack a system.

- **Marketing is disconnected from revenue.** Activity happens, but nobody can say how it ties to leads, jobs, or sales.
- **Priorities are unclear.** Owners do not know which of a dozen possible marketing tasks deserves this week's attention.
- **Content eats time and has no strategy.** Posts get written when someone has a spare hour, about whatever comes to mind.
- **Leads slip through.** Follow-up depends on memory and goodwill rather than a process.
- **The CRM decays.** It becomes an out-of-date address book instead of a tool that runs the sales process.
- **The website leaks.** It attracts visitors but does not turn enough of them into enquiries.
- **Knowledge is scattered.** The truth about the business lives across the website, documents, spreadsheets, inboxes, and people's heads.
- **Vendors do not coordinate.** The owner pays separately for web, content, SEO, ads, automation, and CRM setup, and becomes the unpaid project manager between them.
- **Reports look backwards.** They describe what happened; they do not say what to do next.
- **AI tools produce fragments.** They generate outputs on request, but they do not know the business and they do not complete a process.

The underlying problem is operational: the pieces of growth are not connected, so nobody owns the whole. Braventa exists to address that, not to be one more generic AI writing app.

## 5. Target audience

### Primary audience
- Small and medium-sized businesses, especially **service businesses** with repeatable lead-generation and follow-up processes.
- **Owner-operated** companies without a large internal marketing or operations team.
- Businesses that already use a website, a CRM, email, social platforms, or other business software, and want those tools to work together.
- Businesses that need better systems but cannot justify hiring a team of specialists.

### Buyer and user roles
| Role | What they care about |
|---|---|
| Owner / founder (primary buyer) | Where to focus, whether marketing is worth the money, getting time back |
| Marketing manager or coordinator | A clear plan, faster production, consistent brand voice, less busywork |
| Sales / office manager (CRM user) | Clean pipeline, reliable follow-up (planned capability) |
| Technical administrator | Security, data handling, integrations, permissions |
| Agency or implementation partner | Delivering consistent work across multiple clients (future) |

### Candidate early verticals (hypotheses to validate, not restrictions)
| Vertical | Why it may fit | What would confirm it |
|---|---|---|
| Home and trade services (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, landscaping) | Local, lead-driven, inconsistent marketing, high value per job | Owners complete onboarding and act on opportunities |
| Solar and electrical contractors | Long consideration cycle, education-heavy content, CRM-dependent follow-up | Repeat use of plans and drafts; demand for CRM features |
| Construction and related professional services | Reputation- and referral-led, weak websites, little content | Website opportunities rated useful |
| Marketing-dependent local businesses (clinics, studios, salons) | Social and local search matter; owners stretched thin | Drafts approved with light editing |
| Other service businesses with repeatable acquisition workflows | Same structural problem | Same signals |

No vertical is exclusive until evidence or an explicit founder decision says otherwise (brief §1.6). Copy should say "service businesses" and use vertical examples as illustrations, not as the definition.

## 6. What Braventa is NOT

Braventa is not merely:

| Not this | Why the distinction matters |
|---|---|
| An AI content generator | Content is one output of a plan grounded in the business, not the product itself. |
| A social media scheduler | Scheduling is a delivery step; Braventa decides what is worth posting and why. It does not publish anything today. |
| A CRM replacement | Braventa is designed to make an existing CRM (HubSpot first) work harder, not to replace it. No CRM is connected yet. |
| A website builder | Braventa analyzes a website and recommends improvements; it does not build or host sites. |
| An automation agency | Braventa is software with human approval built in; optional implementation services may come later, but the product is the system. |
| A chatbot with access to business information | Braventa produces structured outputs (profile, score, opportunities, plan, drafts) inside a defined process, not open-ended chat. |

## 8. Competitive frame

Generic categories only. Do not name competitors or make comparative claims about specific products.

| Alternative | What it does well | Where it falls short for our customer | Braventa's angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| General AI writers / chat assistants | Fast text on request | Start from a blank prompt every time; do not know the business; do not decide what matters; no process | Starts from a business profile; decides priorities first, then drafts |
| Social schedulers | Reliable posting calendars | Assume you already know what to post and why | Provides the strategy and the drafts; publishing connections are planned |
| CRMs | System of record for contacts and deals | Often under-maintained; do not tell the owner what to do next | Designed to sit alongside the CRM and make it useful (planned) |
| Agencies and freelancers | Human expertise and execution | Costly, siloed across vendors, slow feedback, knowledge leaves with them | One system that keeps the context, the plan, and the work together; humans stay in control |
| Automation tools | Powerful connectors and triggers | Require someone to design the process and maintain it | Process and recommendations come with the business context (planned) |

Framing rule: we compete with **fragmentation**, not with any one tool. Braventa is the layer that connects the pieces.

## 9. Brand story

Most small businesses run growth the way they had to: one tool at a time, one vendor at a time. A website from one person, social posts from another, a CRM someone set up once, an SEO report nobody acted on. Each piece does its job. Nobody owns the whole.

The owner ends up as the integration layer — the one who remembers what was promised, chases the follow-up, and decides what matters this week, usually late at night and usually by guesswork.

Braventa started as an internal methodology for fixing exactly that: understand the business first, decide what matters most, do the work, measure it, and improve. Braventa turns that methodology into software. Today it reads your website, builds a profile of your business, scores where you stand, picks the five opportunities worth your time, plans the next 30 days of content, and drafts the first posts for you to approve. Next, it will connect to the tools you already use so the plan can move into action and the results can come back.

One system. One place to see what needs to happen. You stay in control of everything that goes out under your name.

## 10. Mission, vision, values

**Vision (brief §1.2).** Help businesses operate more intelligently by connecting their marketing, customer relationships, digital presence, data, and repetitive operational processes into one coherent system.

**Mission (brief §1.3).** Make sophisticated marketing systems, CRM operations, business intelligence, and automation accessible to businesses that cannot afford large internal marketing, technology, and operations teams.

**Core promise (brief §1.4).** Braventa helps businesses understand what needs to happen, turn that understanding into an actionable plan, execute repetitive work through AI-assisted workflows, and improve their systems using data and feedback.

### Values (proposed)

| Value | What it means | Behaviors |
|---|---|---|
| **Own the outcome** | We are accountable for the whole result, not just our piece of it. | Recommendations come with a first step. Failures are reported plainly with what to do next. We do not blame "the AI" or another vendor. |
| **Context before content** | Understanding the business comes before producing anything. | Every output traces back to the business profile. We do not ship features that generate without context. |
| **Say what is true** | Our claims match what the product does today. | "Live" vs "Coming next" is always explicit. No invented stats, testimonials, or guarantees. We show where a number came from (for example, "Google" next to PageSpeed scores). |
| **The customer stays in control** | Nothing goes out under a customer's name without their approval. | Approval is the default. Automation is opt-in and logged. Data is scoped to the customer's organization. |
| **Simple on the surface, rigorous underneath** | Owners should not need to understand the machinery to benefit from it. | Plain-language UI and copy. Sound engineering for security, cost control, and reliability behind it. |

## 12. The Braventa Growth Loop (D-004)

The methodology behind the product. A methodology, not a feature, and never described as fully automated.

| Stage | Steps | Status in the product |
|---|---|---|
| **Understand** | Understand the business · Identify opportunities and bottlenecks · Set priorities and objectives | **Live** (profile, growth score, top five opportunities) |
| **Act** | Build the action plan · Create the assets · Execute through connected workflows | **Partly live** (30-day plan and drafts with approval live; execution planned) |
| **Improve** | Measure results · Learn from outcomes · Improve the strategy and workflows | **Planned** |

Name: "the Braventa Growth Loop" on first mention, "the Growth Loop" after.
